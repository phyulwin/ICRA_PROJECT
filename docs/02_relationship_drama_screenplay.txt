TITLE: THE LAST TRAIN HOME
GENRE: Contemporary Drama / Romance

INT. CITY TRAIN - NIGHT

Rain streaks the windows. LENA ORTIZ, 27, sits beside a suitcase. Her phone shows six missed calls from NOAH.

At the next stop, NOAH REED, 29, soaked from the rain, boards.

NOAH
I checked three stations.

LENA
That sounds inefficient.

NOAH
You weren't answering.

LENA
That was the efficient part.

NOAH
Are you actually leaving?

LENA
The suitcase is mostly decorative.

NOAH
For Boston?

LENA
For the job.

NOAH
That's not what I asked.

LENA
It's the answer you're getting.

INT. CITY TRAIN - CONTINUOUS

The train enters a tunnel.

NOAH
When did you decide?

LENA
This morning.

NOAH
After breakfast?

LENA
Why does that matter?

NOAH
Because I made you breakfast.

LENA
You burned toast.

NOAH
I burned toast while you apparently planned to move six hours away.

Lena fights a smile.

LENA
This is why I didn't want you here.

NOAH
Because of toast?

LENA
Because you make everything feel normal. Then I start thinking maybe normal is enough.

NOAH
Isn't it?

LENA
Not for me. Not right now.

INT. CITY TRAIN - LATER

The train is delayed. Noah takes an envelope from his coat.

NOAH
Open it after I get off.

LENA
That's manipulative.

NOAH
Probably.

LENA
If that's a ring, I'm throwing you off the train.

NOAH
It's not a ring.

LENA
Good.

NOAH
That reaction was devastatingly fast.

She laughs despite herself.

INT. CITY TRAIN - FINAL STOP BEFORE TERMINAL - NIGHT

Noah stands.

LENA
This isn't your stop.

NOAH
I know.

LENA
Then why are you getting off?

NOAH
Because you asked for space.

A beat.

NOAH
Take the job.

LENA
And us?

NOAH
I don't know.

That catches her off guard.

NOAH
If I can talk you out of something you really want, you'll hate me eventually.

The warning tone sounds.

NOAH
Call me when you know what you want.

He exits. The doors close.

INT. CITY TRAIN - MOMENTS LATER

Lena opens the envelope.

Inside: TWO TRAIN TICKETS — BOSTON — dated three months earlier.

On the back:

"I thought we could go see whether you loved it there."

Her phone rings. MOM.

MOM (V.O.)
Did you get on the train?

LENA
Yeah.

MOM (V.O.)
Are you okay?

Lena watches the rainy platform disappear.

LENA
Ask me tomorrow.

FADE OUT.
