TITLE: FAMILY TECH SUPPORT

INT. LIVING ROOM - AFTERNOON

MAYA, 26, enters.

Her MOM holds up a phone.

MOM
The internet is broken.

MAYA
On the phone?

MOM
Everywhere.

Maya checks her own phone. Wi-Fi works perfectly.

MAYA
What happened?

MOM
Nothing. I only changed one thing.

Maya looks concerned.

CUT TO:

INT. HOME OFFICE - MOMENTS LATER

Maya sits at the computer.

The browser is zoomed to 500 percent.

MAYA
Why is everything enormous?

MOM
I wanted the letters bigger.

MAYA
You made the internet bigger.

MOM
Can you make it normal without making the letters small?

Maya stares silently at the screen.

CUT TO:

INT. LIVING ROOM - LATER

Maya fixes the phone.

MAYA
Okay. Everything works.

MOM
What was wrong?

MAYA
Airplane mode was on.

MOM
I didn't turn that on.

Maya looks at her.

Mom looks completely innocent.

MAYA
Of course not.

MOM
Technology does things by itself.

Maya's DAD enters carrying a tablet.

DAD
Since you're here—

Maya immediately grabs her bag.

MAYA
No.

DAD
I haven't even asked yet.

MAYA
I know how this story ends.

CUT TO:

EXT. FRONT PORCH - CONTINUOUS

Maya walks quickly toward her car.

Her phone rings.

CALLER: MOM

Maya stops.

She considers answering.

She declines the call.

A text arrives immediately:

MOM: How do I turn airplane mode off again?

Maya looks up at the sky.

MAYA
I was free for twelve seconds.
