TITLE: FLOOR NINETEEN
GENRE: Tech Thriller

INT. HARTWELL HOTEL - ELEVATOR - NIGHT

NINA SHAW, 32, investigative journalist, watches the numbers rise.

Her phone displays:

UNKNOWN: ROOM 1912. COME ALONE. 11:30. NO RECORDING.

She starts a voice recorder anyway.

The elevator stops at eighteen. Empty hallway.

A hand suddenly blocks the closing doors.

MARCUS BELL, 40s, hotel security, enters and presses 19.

MARCUS
Nineteen?

NINA
Yeah.

He doesn't ask which room.

INT. ROOM 1912 - NIGHT

The door is open. A red folder sits on the desk.

Inside: emails, financial transfers, photographs and dates.

Nina photographs everything.

Her phone vibrates.

UNKNOWN: YOU BROUGHT A RECORDER.

Nina looks around.

UNKNOWN: PUT IT ON THE DESK.

She obeys.

The television switches to a live camera feed of Nina standing in the room.

NINA
Cute.

UNKNOWN: READ PAGE 7.

Page seven contains a photograph of Nina's younger brother entering an office building.

Her confidence vanishes.

NINA
What is this?

UNKNOWN: NOW YOU'RE LISTENING.

INT. ROOM 1912 - LATER

Three knocks.

MARCUS (O.S.)
Hotel security.

NINA
How do you know my name?

Silence.

Marcus enters.

MARCUS
If I wanted to stop you, you wouldn't have gotten upstairs.

NINA
That's comforting.

MARCUS
You have four minutes.

NINA
Until what?

MARCUS
Until whoever monitors those cameras realizes I disabled this floor.

An elevator CHIMES outside.

MARCUS
That's early.

INT. SERVICE STAIRWELL - NIGHT

They hurry downward.

NINA
Who are they?

MARCUS
People who don't want that folder leaving.

NINA
You seem well informed.

MARCUS
Your article about Meridian Logistics named three shell companies.

NINA
There were eleven.

MARCUS
You missed twelve.

He points to the folder.

MARCUS
That's twelve.

INT. HOTEL LAUNDRY FLOOR - NIGHT

Industrial washers rumble.

NINA
Why help me?

MARCUS
My sister worked for Meridian.

NINA
Worked?

He doesn't answer.

A PHONE BUZZES inside the folder.

Nina discovers a second phone taped beneath the cover.

UNKNOWN: MARCUS IS LYING.

She slowly looks at Marcus.

MARCUS
That's inconvenient.

NINA
Which part?

MARCUS
The timing.

He reaches inside his jacket.

Nina shoves a laundry cart between them.

Marcus pulls out a hotel master keycard and tosses it to her.

MARCUS
Exit at the end. Take the loading dock.

NINA
Why should I believe you?

MARCUS
You shouldn't. But you should move.

Footsteps approach.

INT. HOTEL LOADING DOCK - NIGHT

Nina runs into the rain.

The hidden phone buzzes.

UNKNOWN: DO NOT PUBLISH THE FOLDER.

Nina types:

THEN WHY GIVE IT TO ME?

A reply appears.

UNKNOWN: BECAUSE I NEED YOU TO FIND WHAT'S MISSING.

Across the street, a parked car turns on its headlights.

Nina steps backward into shadow.

CUT TO BLACK.
