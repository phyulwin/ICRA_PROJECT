TITLE: CORPORATE SURVIVAL MODE

INT. STARTUP OFFICE - MORNING

JAMIE, 24, walks in carrying coffee and trying to look awake.

His manager, PRIYA, appears beside him.

PRIYA
Quick question.

Jamie freezes. He slowly lowers the coffee.

JAMIE
How quick?

PRIYA
Can you present the quarterly report in ten minutes?

Jamie stares into the distance.

JAMIE
The report I haven't seen?

PRIYA
That's the one.

Jamie nods with fake confidence.

JAMIE
Love a challenge.

Priya leaves. Jamie immediately opens his laptop.

JAMIE
I do not love a challenge.

CUT TO:

INT. CONFERENCE ROOM - LATER

A presentation slide reads: Q3 RESULTS.

Jamie stands before six executives.

JAMIE
As you can clearly see—

The screen changes to Jamie's search history:

"how to understand quarterly report fast"

Silence.

Jamie slowly turns toward the screen.

EXECUTIVE
Interesting research methodology.

JAMIE
Transparency is one of our core values.

CUT TO:

INT. OFFICE KITCHEN - AFTERNOON

Jamie stares blankly into a microwave while his lunch spins.

COWORKER
How did the presentation go?

JAMIE
I survived.

The microwave BEEPS.

Jamie opens it.

He forgot to put the food inside.

Jamie closes the microwave again.

JAMIE
Debatable.
