TITLE: FIRST DATE DISASTER

EXT. RESTAURANT ENTRANCE - NIGHT

ELLA, 23, checks her phone.

TEXT FROM DATE: I'm wearing black.

Ella looks up.

Almost everyone entering the restaurant is wearing black.

ELLA
Excellent clue.

A MAN waves in her direction.

Ella smiles and waves enthusiastically.

The man walks past her and hugs someone behind her.

Ella converts the wave into an awkward stretch.

ELLA
Perfect recovery.

CUT TO:

INT. RESTAURANT - NIGHT

Ella finally sits across from DANIEL, 25.

DANIEL
I was worried I'd be awkward.

ELLA
Don't worry. I'm extremely normal.

She immediately knocks over her water.

Both stare at it spreading across the table.

ELLA
That was a demonstration.

DANIEL
Of?

ELLA
Gravity.

CUT TO:

INT. RESTAURANT - LATER

The server places the bill down.

Ella and Daniel both reach for it.

ELLA
I'll get it.

DANIEL
No, I invited you.

They politely fight over the bill.

The server stands there waiting.

Their smiles become increasingly aggressive.

ELLA
I insist.

DANIEL
So do I.

SERVER
I can split it.

Both immediately release the bill.

ELLA
Perfect.

DANIEL
Great idea.

They avoid eye contact.

CUT TO:

EXT. RESTAURANT - NIGHT

DANIEL
I actually had fun.

ELLA
Me too.

A pause.

They attempt a goodbye hug, choose opposite directions, switch, then switch again.

Finally they shake hands.

DANIEL
Very romantic.

ELLA
I'll never recover from this.
