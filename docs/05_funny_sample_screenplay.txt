TITLE: THE PRESENTATION INCIDENT

INT. UNIVERSITY CLASSROOM - DAY

SAM, 20, presents beside a projector.

SAM
As we can see from this graph—

He clicks. Instead of a graph, a giant confused-cat reaction image fills the screen.

Silence.

PROFESSOR HARRIS
Is this part of the economic model?

SAM
Technically... no.

He clicks again. Another accidental slide appears: a reaction meme about avoiding eye contact when a professor asks questions.

The class turns toward Professor Harris.

SAM
I can explain.

TARA
Can you?

SAM
No.

PROFESSOR HARRIS
Please continue your academic research.

The class laughs. Sam finally finds the correct graph.

SAM
Pretend the last thirty seconds didn't happen.

PROFESSOR HARRIS
Impossible.

A notification appears on the projector:

MOM: GOOD LUCK ON YOUR PRESENTATION SWEETIE!

The class collectively goes "Aww."

SAM
I'm dropping out.

TARA
No. This is your strongest presentation ever.

Professor Harris writes something down.

SAM
Is that my grade?

PROFESSOR HARRIS
I'm documenting the incident for history.

SAM
Fair.
