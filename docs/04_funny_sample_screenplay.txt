TITLE: MAIN CHARACTER COMMUTE

INT. CITY TRAIN - MORNING

LEAH, 22, watches her reflection in the train window and adjusts her jacket.

LEAH
Okay. Main character energy.

She turns dramatically toward the window just as the train jerks. Leah stumbles directly into a pole.

A TEENAGER sees everything.

TEENAGER
You good?

LEAH
That was choreography.

Her phone rings. MOM.

MOM (PHONE)
Did you bring your lunch?

Leah checks her empty bag.

LEAH
Yes.

CUT TO: Her lunch sitting on the kitchen counter.

MOM (PHONE)
Are you lying?

LEAH
Why would you ask that?

MOM (PHONE)
Because I'm looking at it.

The teenager tries not to laugh.

LEAH
This conversation is private.

TEENAGER
I'm not involved and somehow I'm embarrassed.

The doors open. Leah steps off with dignity. Her tote catches on the door. She yanks it free.

TEENAGER
Main character!

The doors close.

LEAH
My aura will recover.
