TITLE: I CAN'T DO IT
INT. APARTMENT KITCHEN - NIGHT
AVA stares dramatically at one dirty plate. LEO enters.
LEO
What happened?
AVA
I can't do this anymore.
LEO
Wash one plate?
AVA
Don't minimize my journey.
She rolls up her sleeves like she's entering battle and turns on the faucet. Water sprays directly onto her shirt. She freezes.
AVA
The universe has made its position clear.
LEO
It's one plate.
AVA
Today it's one plate. Tomorrow it's taxes.
Leo washes it in five seconds.
AVA
Wow.
Leo notices an overflowing laundry basket.
LEO
No.
AVA
See? Now you understand.
