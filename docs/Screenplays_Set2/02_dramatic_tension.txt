EXT. ROOFTOP - NIGHT

Rain beats against the rooftop.

MARA stands near the edge while ELI approaches cautiously.

ELI
You disappeared for three years.

MARA
I had a reason.

ELI
Then say it.

Mara turns. She wants to speak, but stops when distant sirens begin below.

MARA
If I tell you, you can't go home tonight.

Eli studies her face.

ELI
Then I guess I'm not going home.

A long silence.

Mara finally removes a small silver key from her coat pocket and places it in his hand.

MARA
They already know you have this.

Eli looks down at the key, then back at Mara.

The rooftop door suddenly SLAMS open.
