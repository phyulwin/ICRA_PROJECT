INT. LIBRARY - AFTERNOON

NINA sits alone at a wooden table beside a tall window.

She turns the final page of a handwritten letter.

Outside, leaves move gently in the wind.

Nina reads the last sentence again.

She folds the letter carefully and places it inside an old book.

A librarian pushes a cart quietly between the shelves.

Nina closes her eyes for a moment.

When she opens them, she looks calmer.

She stands, returns the book to the shelf, and walks toward the exit.

At the doorway, she pauses and looks back once.

Then she leaves.
