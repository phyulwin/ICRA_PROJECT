TITLE: LOCKED IN

INT. STARTUP OFFICE - MORNING

ELI, 24, enters carrying an enormous iced coffee. PRIYA, 25, is already working.

ELI
Do not speak to me today. I'm locked in.

He puts on headphones. His monitor shows a blank presentation titled FINAL_FINAL_REAL_v7. Eli types one word: STRATEGY. He stares at it.

PRIYA
Incredible progress.

ELI
Respect the process.

His phone buzzes. He checks one notification, then another, then a short video.

TWENTY MINUTES LATER

PRIYA
Locked in?

ELI
This is research.

PRIYA
You're watching a raccoon steal a sandwich.

ELI
Competitor analysis.

Their manager DANIEL approaches.

DANIEL
How's the presentation?

Eli instantly flips his phone facedown.

ELI
Excellent. We're refining the strategic framework.

Priya looks at the single word on his screen.

DANIEL
Great. Show me.

Eli freezes.

PRIYA
He's been cooking. Unfortunately, nothing is cooked.

Daniel silently walks away.

ELI
Why would you expose me?

PRIYA
You exposed yourself in 4K.

Eli reaches for his coffee. It is still completely full.

ELI
Today has been difficult.
