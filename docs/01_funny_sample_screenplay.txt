TITLE: AURA BANKRUPTCY

INT. COLLEGE LIBRARY - AFTERNOON

JAY, 20, enters wearing sunglasses indoors. MAYA, 21, watches him approach.

MAYA
Why are you walking like you own the building?

JAY
I don't walk. I arrive.

Jay spins a rolling chair around and attempts to sit backward. The chair rolls away. Jay drops to the floor. Three students slowly look over their laptops.

MAYA
Minus eight thousand aura.

JAY
That wasn't canon.

Jay stands and casually leans against a bookshelf. A book immediately falls onto his shoulder.

MAYA
The aura economy is collapsing.

JAY
Temporary market correction.

He opens his laptop. BATTERY: 2%.

JAY
Charger?

MAYA
You came here with two percent?

JAY
I believed in myself.

The laptop dies.

MAYA
Generational aura debt.

Jay removes his sunglasses.

LATER

Jay sits on the floor beside the only outlet. Maya photographs him.

JAY
Delete that.

MAYA
No. Character development.

A passing student gives Jay a sympathetic nod.

JAY
We're rebuilding.
