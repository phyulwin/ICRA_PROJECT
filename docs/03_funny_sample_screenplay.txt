TITLE: THE GROUP CHAT TRIAL

INT. APARTMENT LIVING ROOM - NIGHT

NORA, DEV, LUIS, and KIM sit around a coffee table.

NORA
We need to discuss what happened in the group chat.

DEV
I invoke my right to remain mysterious.

NORA
At 2:14 A.M. you sent seventeen messages.

DEV
Allegedly.

Kim holds up her phone.

KIM
They're literally right here.

DEV
AI generated.

LUIS
One is a picture of your refrigerator.

DEV
Deepfake.

NORA
Then you wrote, "Nobody understands me except this fridge."

DEV
Context matters.

KIM
What context makes that normal?

DEV
It keeps my food cold without asking questions.

Luis nods.

LUIS
Low-key loyal.

NORA
Do not encourage him.

Dev leans back confidently. His chair CRACKS and slowly collapses. He disappears below the table.

LUIS
The universe fact-checked you.

DEV
I'm still standing spiritually.

KIM
You are horizontal physically.

Nora takes a photo.

DEV
Don't put that in the group chat.

All their phones immediately ping.

DEV
You people move with terrifying efficiency.
