TITLE: PITCH PERFECTLY WRONG
GENRE: Workplace Comedy

INT. BRIGHTLINE STARTUP - CONFERENCE ROOM - MORNING

A glass conference room overlooks a crowded office. MAYA CHEN, 28, product lead, checks a presentation while DEREK VALE, 31, founder, adjusts his blazer.

MAYA
Please tell me you changed slide twelve.

DEREK
Absolutely.

MAYA
You said that too quickly.

Maya clicks. A gigantic photo of Derek riding a mechanical bull appears beneath the words: TAM: BASICALLY EVERYONE.

Silence.

DEREK
That's... not the updated deck.

PRIYA, 34, finance director, lowers her coffee.

PRIYA
What was the updated slide?

DEREK
Same numbers. Less bull.

The door opens. Investor ELLIOT PARK enters with two associates.

ELLIOT
Hope we're not early.

DEREK
You're exactly on time.

MAYA
Unfortunately.

INT. BRIGHTLINE STARTUP - CONFERENCE ROOM - LATER

DEREK
Our platform removes friction from everyday decision-making.

He introduces CHOOSR, an AI product that recommends meals, clothes and activities.

ELLIOT
People don't know what they want?

DEREK
Have you ever watched two people decide where to eat?

Elliot almost smiles.

A notification suddenly covers the presentation.

MOM: Derek, did you remember your dentist appointment???

Derek freezes. Another appears.

MOM: I KNOW YOU SAW THIS.

Priya stares at him.

DEREK
Contextual intelligence.

Maya lunges for the laptop.

ELLIOT
Strong parental engagement.

DEREK
Our earliest investor.

INT. BRIGHTLINE STARTUP - KITCHEN - AFTERNOON

The team debriefs.

MAYA
You told an investor your mother was our earliest investor.

DEREK
She gave me eight hundred dollars when I was nineteen.

PRIYA
For the company?

DEREK
For textbooks.

PRIYA
Did you buy textbooks?

Derek drinks.

Maya notices Elliot through the glass wall. He can hear everything.

She points subtly. Derek follows her eyes.

Elliot raises his coffee in greeting.

Derek's smile remains frozen for a long beat.

DEREK
Great acoustics.

INT. BRIGHTLINE STARTUP - OPEN OFFICE - EVENING

Maya reads an email.

DEREK
How bad?

MAYA
He wants a second meeting.

Derek lights up.

MAYA
With me.

His smile collapses.

MAYA
I'm kidding.

DEREK
That wasn't funny.

MAYA
It was extremely funny.

She shows him the email.

DEREK
He liked the product.

MAYA
He liked that we recovered when everything went wrong.

DEREK
That's leadership.

MAYA
That's survival.

A beat.

DEREK
Should we keep the bull slide?

Maya stares at him.

CUT TO BLACK.
