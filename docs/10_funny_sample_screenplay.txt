TITLE: SOCIAL BATTERY
INT. HOUSE PARTY - NIGHT
Music blasts. SOPHIE smiles politely.
FRIEND
Having fun?
SOPHIE
So much fun.
Her smile vanishes the second her friend turns away. Sophie checks the time: 9:07 PM.
SOPHIE
I've been here seven minutes.
Someone across the room waves. Sophie enthusiastically waves back. They were waving at someone behind her. Sophie turns the wave into fixing her hair.
SOPHIE
Nobody saw that.
STRANGER
I saw it.
LATER: Sophie hides alone in the kitchen eating chips.
FRIEND
Come meet everyone!
Sophie looks toward the crowd like a soldier returning to battle.
SOPHIE
Tell my family I loved them.
Her phone buzzes.
MOM: Want me to call and pretend there's an emergency?
Sophie's eyes light up.
SOPHIE
My extraction team is ready.
