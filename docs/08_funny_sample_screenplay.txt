TITLE: THE AWKWARD REUNION
INT. GROCERY STORE - AFTERNOON
MARCUS turns a corner and sees EMILY, an old classmate. Both recognize each other and immediately pretend they don't.
Marcus becomes intensely interested in canned beans. Emily studies a rice bag like it contains classified information.
They accidentally enter the same aisle.
EMILY
Oh! Hey!
MARCUS
Whoa! Didn't even see you.
EMILY
How have you been?
MARCUS
Amazing. Really busy.
His cart contains frozen pizza and twelve instant noodles.
EMILY
Hosting?
MARCUS
Approximately.
Long silence. They repeatedly nod.
EMILY
Well...
MARCUS
Yeah...
They walk away, both look back at the same time, then instantly face forward.
MARCUS
Perfectly normal interaction.
