TITLE: THE EXPERT
INT. OFFICE BREAK ROOM - DAY
RINA struggles with a coffee machine. OWEN enters.
OWEN
Need help?
RINA
I've used these a thousand times.
She presses a button. The machine starts cleaning itself.
OWEN
Interesting choice.
RINA
Supposed to happen.
The machine makes a terrible grinding sound.
OWEN
Still supposed to happen?
RINA
Absolutely.
A warning flashes: ADD WATER.
Rina fills it and presses START. Nothing happens.
Owen silently reaches over and plugs the machine into the wall. It immediately turns on.
They stare at each other.
RINA
I was testing you.
OWEN
Did I pass?
RINA
Unfortunately.
